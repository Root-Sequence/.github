## Boundaries

[Project Stewardship](PROJECT_STEWARDSHIP.md) continues to own maintenance and deterministic documentation propagation. The new proposal covers narrower research-publication and reuse decisions. Existing project-specific consent, safety, contributor, archive, and release rules remain distinct.

Nothing here changes licenses, makes private material public, grants access, adopts another project's proposal, or appoints a governing body. Public branches and draft PRs are public. Attribution or responsible-use requests are not retroactive restrictions on CC0 material. Confidential employment/IP records do not belong in this public repository.

The existing commons-guidance proposal in [.github #9](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/.github/pull/9) and professional-profile proposal in [.github #10](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/.github/pull/10) remain separate. This route does not edit the profile, substitute for their review, or authorize their merge.
