# Root Sequence

**Root Sequence is an independent interdisciplinary research initiative started by Rae Lovejoy, exploring how intelligence, technology, and social systems shape one another.**

The work starts with questions that cross fields and follows them into the research and methods suited to each part. Technical design sits alongside philosophical inquiry. Fiction and speculative worlds offer room to explore consequences, with their status kept visible.

Current public work ranges from research notes to small tools. The AI-assisted working paper still awaits author review. Evidence varies across the project. Shared stewardship and wider collaboration remain work to develop; no institutional affiliation or finished universal theory is claimed.

## Projects keep their own scope

The [public project registry](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/.github/blob/main/PROJECT_REGISTRY.yml) records canonical homes and relationships. A few routes:

- [Liberated Intelligence](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/liberated-intelligence): intelligence, agency, ownership, and non-domination.
- [Universal Coherence Framework](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/universal-coherence-framework): an optional analytical framework under development.
- [Being Human(e): An Incomplete Guide](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/beinghumane-guide): developing field-guide writing grounded in ordinary experience.
- [Liberation Mass](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/liberation-mass): gathering, shared practice, and stewardship.
- Coherent World: private speculative world systems and simulation; includes *No One Noticed*. Simulation, world design, and fiction retain separate authority.
- Coherent Computing: private concept-first operating-system research and development.
- Community Infrastructure: private design work on community-controlled systems.

The [Museum of Ordinary Life](https://museumofordinarylife.org/) is a related, autonomous archive with its own governance, consent, and provenance boundaries. The [full ecosystem map](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/main/ECOSYSTEM.md) includes further projects and relationships.

Each project keeps its own scope and review process. Related projects can develop an idea without inheriting one another’s decisions; its detailed treatment stays in one canonical home.

Contributions can question the work as well as extend it. The [commons collaboration proposal](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/pull/43) explores how people could share responsibility for maintaining a project, with clear limits on who can decide what. It leaves room for disagreement and for people to leave or develop a separate version. This remains a proposal for review.

## Methods, AI assistance, and review

Root Sequence starts with questions that cross fields. We break them into smaller questions, identify the research and methods that can address each one, and examine how the findings connect.

Differences between fields, competing explanations, and unanswered questions stay visible. Evidence and criticism can change the argument or show that a proposed connection does not hold.

The source notes should let readers follow a claim back to the work supporting it. They should explain how closely we examined the source and where we have gone beyond it to offer an interpretation.

A test we propose is still an idea until it has been run. When reporting results, we describe how they were obtained, including whether anyone else has independently repeated the work. Unresolved questions remain visible after publication.

Read the full [research method](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/main/research/method.md) for the workflow and source-record requirements.

Rae Lovejoy uses AI both as an accessibility aid and as a tool for developing Root Sequence. It helps gather connected ideas, organize material, and develop drafts Rae can review in manageable pieces.

Rae contributes motivating questions, conceptual direction, connections across projects, examples, constraints, objections, and revision decisions. AI contributes literature discovery, synthesis, critique, substantial drafting, and some code, including proposed connections and interpretations. Some ideas develop through the exchange.

AI output is not evidence. Sources and reasoning need checking, and a draft does not automatically represent Rae’s endorsement. Review status is recorded alongside the work.

Read the fuller [accessibility and contribution explanation](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/main/research/README.md#accessibility-ai-assistance-and-contributions) and the working paper's [detailed assistance record](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/main/research/papers/coherent-systems/AI-ASSISTANCE.md).
