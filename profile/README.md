# [root]sequence

Root Sequence is a systems-oriented lab for research, experimentation, and exploratory writing.

It explores root structures and unfolding sequences: the foundations, relationships, and processes that shape systems, intelligence, technology, and reality.

## Scope

Root Sequence is a home for work that is conceptual, connective, and systems-focused.

It exists as a place to:

- explore systems in theory  
- trace patterns across domains  
- develop frameworks, concepts, and models  
- publish experimental writing and research  
- connect technical, philosophical, and social lines of thought  

## What lives here

This organization contains work related to:

- systems research  
- conceptual frameworks  
- exploratory writing  
- experimental tools  
- philosophical and technical synthesis  
- long-term, evolving projects  

## Relationship to Dev11

Root Sequence explores systems in theory. Dev11 builds them in practice.

Root Sequence focuses on structure, emergence, and meaning.  
Dev11 focuses on implementation, tooling, and execution.

Root Sequence is active, evolving, and intentionally unfinished.
