# [root]sequence

Root Sequence is a systems-oriented lab for research, experimentation, and exploratory writing.

It focuses on root structures and unfolding sequences: the foundations, relationships, and processes that shape systems, intelligence, technology, and reality.

## Get to the root

Root Sequence is a home for work that is more conceptual, connective, and systems-focused.

It exists as a place to:

- explore systems in theory
- trace patterns across domains
- develop frameworks, concepts, and models
- publish experimental writing and research
- connect technical, philosophical, and social lines of thought

## What lives here

This organization contains projects related to:

- systems research
- conceptual frameworks
- exploratory writing
- experimental tools
- philosophical and technical synthesis
- long-term, evolving bodies of work

## Relationship to Dev11

Root Sequence explores systems in theory. Dev11 builds them in practice.

Where Root Sequence is more concerned with structure, emergence, and meaning, Dev11 is more focused on implementation, tooling, and technical execution.
